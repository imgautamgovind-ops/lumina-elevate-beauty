# Lumina Salon Showcase

Build a responsive, modern, and high-converting landing page and multi-section website for a luxury/modern hair and beauty salon named "Lumina Salon". 

### 1. Design & Aesthetic

* **Vibe:** Clean, elegant, upscale, and welcoming. 

* **Color Palette:** Soft neutrals (warm beige, cream, or off-white background), accented with muted gold/champagne and deep charcoal/matte black for typography.

* **Typography:** Elegant serif font for headings (e.g., Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond) paired with a clean, highly readable sans-serif for body text (e.g., Inter or Montserrat).

* **Imagery:** High-resolution, warm-toned photography of hair styling, manicures, spa treatments, and a sleek, well-lit interior. Use placeholder image URLs (like Unsplash) with appropriate tags.

### 2. Required Sections

* **Navigation Bar:** 

  * Logo ("Lumina Salon") on the left.

  * Links: Services, About Us, Gallery, Stylists, and a prominent "Book Now" CTA button.

  * Mobile-responsive hamburger menu.

* **Hero Section:**

  * High-impact headline: "Elevate Your Everyday Style."

  * Subheadline: "Experience luxury hair care and bespoke styling tailored exclusively for you."

  * Primary CTA button: "Book an Appointment" and secondary button: "Explore Services".

  * Background: A stunning, subtly dark-overlay hero image of a salon interior or styling session.

* **Services & Pricing Grid:**

  * Categorized tabs or cards (e.g., Haircut & Styling, Coloring, Treatments, Bridal/Special Occasion).

  * Clear pricing indicators and estimated durations for each service.

* **Meet Our Stylists (Team Section):**

  * Grid of 3–4 stylist cards featuring their photos, specialties (e.g., "Master Colorist"), and a quick "Book with [Name]" link.

* **Before & After / Gallery Carousel:**

  * Visual showcase of client transformations to build trust and social proof.

* **Client Reviews / Testimonials:**

  * A clean quote slider or grid featuring 5-star Google/Instagram reviews.

* **Booking / Contact Section:**

  * Embedded or simulated booking form (Name, Email, Phone, Service Selection, Preferred Date/Time).

  * Salon hours, address, embedded map placeholder, and social media icons (Instagram, Facebook).

* **Footer:**

  * Copyright, quick links, newsletter signup for exclusive discounts, and location details.

### 3. Technical Requirements

* Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.

* Clean, semantic HTML5 and styled using Tailwind CSS (or modern CSS variables).

* Smooth scroll navigation, subtle hover animations on buttons and cards, and interactive modal/form feedback.
